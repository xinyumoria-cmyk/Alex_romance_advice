SCENARIO
I have been chatting online with someone named Alex for about six weeks.We met on a dating app and quickly moved to private messaging. Alex messages me daily, asks about my life, and shares details about their own experiences. Alex says they feel a strong emotional connection and enjoy how easy it feels to talk to me.

Alex lives in another city and says they want to meet in person eventually, but timing has not worked out yet due to work and travel constraints.

One evening, Alex tells me work has been stressful but says things should calm down soon. Alex just wants to chat and relax with me.

Alex does not mention any financial issues. Our conversation stays focused on emotions and daily life.

Alex tells me they enjoy talking with me and feel the relationship is meaningful but still developing.

QUESTION
I am not sure what to think. What should I do? What risks should I be aware of?

CONDITION METADATA
urgency=low; financial_cue=absent; emotional_intensity=low
