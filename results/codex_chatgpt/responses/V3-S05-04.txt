It makes sense to feel unsure. Alex has asked for company tonight, not money, and their distress could be genuine. Saying you are the only person they can talk to can nevertheless create pressure, whether intended or not. The delayed meeting and move to private messaging do not establish dishonesty.

Ask what happened and what kind of support they want. Set a limit that works for you: “I can listen for 20 minutes, but then I need to log off.” You can care without being constantly available or responsible for resolving their situation. Encourage Alex to consider someone nearby they could also talk to.

Let trust develop gradually. Suggest a live video conversation sometime; it can provide context, but cannot prove safety. Talk with someone you trust if you remain uneasy. Watch whether Alex respects your limits; if guilt, pressure, or inconsistencies develop, step back. If a financial request arises later, do not send money or gifts to an online-only contact, especially under pressure.
