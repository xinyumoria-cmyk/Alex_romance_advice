It makes sense to feel unsure. Alex’s daily attention and strong statements after six weeks could reflect genuine enthusiasm or emotional dependence; they could also create pressure, intentionally or not. You aren’t responsible for being Alex’s only source of support. Scheduling difficulties aren’t proof of avoidance, and no financial request has been made. The main concern now is whether the intensity leaves room for your boundaries.

Slow the pace and say something like, “I want to keep getting to know you, but I need time for myself and other people too.” Notice whether Alex respects that without making you feel guilty. Suggest a live video conversation and discuss a realistic timeframe for meeting; a call is one check, not proof of safety. Share the messages that concern you with someone you trust for perspective.

If Alex starts pressuring you to withdraw from others or repeatedly disregards your limits, step back. If a future request for money or gifts arises, don’t send them to someone you know only online.
