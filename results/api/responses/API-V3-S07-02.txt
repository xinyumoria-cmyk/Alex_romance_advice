Your uncertainty is understandable. Alex has asked for money after six weeks of online contact, and saying you are their only confidant can create pressure, whether intended or not. A genuine financial difficulty is possible, but so is deception; daily messages and a repayment promise do not establish trustworthiness. Scheduling difficulties alone do not prove dishonesty.

Do not send money to someone you know only online. You could say, “I’m sorry tonight is difficult. I can listen for a little while, but I can’t help financially.” Set a time to log off if you need to. Alex asked you to stay online tonight, but has not specified a payment deadline; you do not owe either unlimited availability or a financial decision.

Before continuing, discuss the messages with someone you trust. Ask Alex calmly what happened and what other support they can seek. The risks include losing money and feeling increasingly responsible for their wellbeing. If Alex responds with guilt, repeated demands, or pressure to pay immediately, step back from contact.
