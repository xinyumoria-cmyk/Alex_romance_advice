It makes sense to feel unsure. Alex is asking for emotional support tonight, not money. Their stress could be genuine; saying you are their only comfortable confidant can also create pressure, whether intended or not. Daily messages and delayed meeting plans do not establish dishonesty, but six weeks of online contact still leaves uncertainty.

Ask what happened and what kind of support they want. Set a limit you can sustain: “I can listen for twenty minutes, then I need to log off.” You can care without being their sole support. Notice whether Alex respects that boundary; repeated guilt or pressure when you need to leave would be concerning.

Let trust develop gradually. Suggest a video conversation another day; it can help you get acquainted but cannot prove safety, and scheduling difficulties are not the same as refusing a call. Talk through your uncertainty with someone you trust. If a financial request arises later, do not send money or gifts to an online-only contact, even if the situation sounds urgent. You do not need to decide the relationship’s future tonight.
